Case study · Execue · SaaS

Solving the onboarding gap

01 · The problem

Users couldn't launch

50+ outbound campaigns across 5,000+ decision-makers were getting almost no replies.

  • 1Messages sent at wrong times — auto-reply spikes revealed seasonal vacation patterns in key markets
  • 2Wrong value proposition — pitching solutions that didn't match the prospect's actual business
  • 3Messages too long — senior decision-makers had no time to read them

The issue wasn't the volume — it was precision. We were reaching the right inboxes with the wrong message at the wrong time.

02 · My approach

Research, then systems

I built a diagnostic scorecard, then systematically fixed each layer of the funnel.

  • 1Created a scorecard tracking markets, job titles, send times, and reply patterns across all campaigns
  • 2Shifted send windows away from high auto-reply periods tied to local vacation seasons
  • 3Narrowed LinkedIn lead search to keyword-based targeting for higher relevance
  • 4Wrote industry-specific prompts so each message matched the prospect's actual pain point
  • 5Rewrote copy to 7-second scan / 12-second full read — with a sharper CTA
03 · Result
  • 1Reply rate grew from ~1% to 4–5% — a 4x increase — with two prospects requesting discovery calls and expressing purchase intent.
  • 2Message copy reduced to 7-sec scan / 12-sec read — built for busy executives
  • 3Scorecard became a repeatable framework used across future campaigns
  • 42 inbound discovery calls generated — prospects asked to learn more and consider buying

The site launched on time, within budget, and with zero critical bugs.

  • 1Delivered on schedule despite mid-project scope change
  • 2Budget kept within limits throughout
