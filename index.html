Case study · Execue · SaaS

Solving the onboarding gap

01 · The problem

Users couldn't launch

50+ outbound campaigns across 5,000+ decision-makers were getting almost no replies.

  • 1Messages sent at wrong times — auto-reply spikes revealed seasonal vacation patterns in key markets
  • 2Wrong value proposition — pitching solutions that didn't match the prospect's actual business
  • 3Messages too long — senior decision-makers had no time to read them

The issue wasn't the volume — it was precision. We were reaching the right inboxes with the wrong message at the wrong time.

02 · My approach

Research, then systems

I built a diagnostic scorecard, then systematically fixed each layer of the funnel.

  • 1Created a scorecard tracking markets, job titles, send times, and reply patterns across all campaigns
  • 2Shifted send windows away from high auto-reply periods tied to local vacation seasons
  • 3Narrowed LinkedIn lead search to keyword-based targeting for higher relevance
  • 4Wrote industry-specific prompts so each message matched the prospect's actual pain point
  • 5Rewrote copy to 7-second scan / 12-second full read — with a sharper CTA
03 · Result
  • 1Reply rate grew from ~1% to 4–5% — a 4x increase — with two prospects requesting discovery calls and expressing purchase intent.
  • 2Message copy reduced to 7-sec scan / 12-sec read — built for busy executives
  • 3Scorecard became a repeatable framework used across future campaigns
  • 42 inbound discovery calls generated — prospects asked to learn more and consider buying

I took on a dual role — Lead Designer and project coordinator — bridging the client and the dev team.

Case study · Avon · E-commerce

From 0.7% to 1.4%

01 · The problem

Customers couldn't complete orders their way

my.avon.ua lacked Nova Poshta parcel lockers, cash-on-delivery, and several post office options — all standard expectations for Ukrainian shoppers.

  • 1Conversion rate of just 0.7% — well below market expectations
  • 2High inbound call volume to the support hotline from customers unable to complete checkout
  • 3Low customer satisfaction scores
  • 4The only developer who owned the custom checkout was mobilised — leaving no internal path forward

The project needed fast cross-team coordination and a creative technical workaround to reach the finish line.

02 · My approach

Research, alignment, and offshore execution

I took full ownership of delivery — from business case to deployment — bridging leadership, an India-based backend team, and the product.

  • 1Identified the gap, assessed the risk, and escalated to leadership with a clear business case and budget proposal
  • 2Discovered the same functionality already existed on the Representatives' site — shared this with the backend team to accelerate development and reduce cost
  • 3Wrote a detailed incident brief with exact requirements, expected behaviour, and edge cases for the offshore team
  • 4Attended every development sync to track progress, align on changes, and unblock the team
  • 5Coordinated QA and co-led the final deployment to production
03 · Result
  • 1Conversion rate doubled — from 0.7% to 1.4% — within the first month of launch
  • 250 parcel locker orders placed in the first month, directly attributable to the new feature
  • 3New delivery options contributed an estimated 15–20% revenue uplift in month one
  • 4Inbound support calls decreased as customers could now self-serve through checkout
  • 5Delivered on time and within budget — despite losing the primary developer mid-project
