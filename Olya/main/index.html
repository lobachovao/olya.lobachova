Case study

This case study highlights how direct user communication and campaign support helped identify common SaaS onboarding challenges and improve outreach quality.

Customer Problem

Many SaaS users struggled to launch effective outbound campaigns.

Common issues included:

  • 1Unclear messaging in cold outreach
  • 2Difficulty structuring campaigns
  • 3Slow onboarding and confusion during setup

As a result, some users delayed launching campaigns or requested frequent support.

My approach

I worked directly with users to identify pain points and improve their outreach process.

Key actions included:

  • 1Communicating with clients and collecting feedback
  • 2Creating scorecards to evaluate outreach message quality
  • 3Helping users structure campaigns
  • 4Experimenting with AI-assisted message generation
  • 5Supporting campaign setup in Instantly and HeyReach
Result

These efforts helped users launch campaigns faster and improve outreach quality.

Key outcomes:

  • 1Clearer messaging strategies for clients
  • 2Faster onboarding and campaign activation
  • 3Valuable user insights for product improvements.

Case study

This case study shows how I led the end-to-end launch of a European e-commerce website — managing the team, budget, and scope changes while keeping the project on track.

Challenge

Launching an e-commerce site across European markets with a tight deadline and fixed budget.

Key constraints:

  • 1Cross-functional team of 5 with no dedicated PM
  • 2Mid-project design requirements changed by the client
  • 3Risk of scope creep threatening the launch date

The project needed both design leadership and hands-on coordination to reach the finish line.

My approach

I took on a dual role — Lead Designer and project coordinator — bridging the client and the dev team.

Key actions:

  • 1Briefing developers and translating business requirements into clear tasks
  • 2Tracking budget and flagging risks early
  • 3Assessing the real cost of mid-project design changes
  • 4Negotiating a compromise: partial changes now, the rest in the next release
  • 5Keeping the team focused while managing client expectations
Result

The site launched on time, within budget, and with zero critical bugs.

Key outcomes:

  • 1Delivered on schedule despite mid-project scope change
  • 2Budget kept within limits throughout
  • 30 critical bugs at launch
  • 4Client continued working with the team after release
